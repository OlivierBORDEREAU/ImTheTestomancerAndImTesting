# Project-specific rules

**There are NO active project overrides in this bundled file.** It imposes no runtime budget, coverage threshold, naming convention, reporter, browser/device matrix, database, or compliance requirement.

Discover active constraints from the user's request and the target repository's instructions, manifests, configuration, and CI. Record their source and scope; clarify material conflicts rather than inventing policy. Project policy must not override higher-priority safety constraints or turn unexecuted tests into verified results.

The [project-policy example](../assets/project-policy.example.md) is an **inactive template**, not a policy to apply automatically. A project owner may copy and adapt it to an agreed location in their own repository and explicitly activate it. Do not edit this portable skill to smuggle one project's policy into every consumer.
